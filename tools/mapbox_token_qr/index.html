Mapbox token to comma

Turn your Mapbox public token into a QR code, then scan it with the comma's driver camera.

The QR code appears here
  1. On the comma: settings, navigation, mapbox token.
  2. Turn the phone's brightness up and hold it 20 to 30 cm in front of the driver camera.
  3. Keep it still until the comma says the token is saved.

The token never leaves this page: the QR code is drawn in your browser and the page can't send anything. Only use a public token (pk.), restricted to the Directions and Search APIs.
